    How to play

    Gardner's 5×5 minichess with quantum moves. Every square is a qubit; the engine is a real Qiskit statevector.

    • Move as in chess. Sliding past a translucent "maybe" piece entangles your piece with it.
    • Split a non-pawn piece onto two empty squares: it is on both, 50% each.
    • Merge two parts of one piece onto one square: their amplitudes interfere.
    • Capture triggers a measurement: the board decides where pieces really are, and the capture succeeds or fizzles.
    • No check: capture the king to win. No castling or en passant. Pawns promote to queens.
